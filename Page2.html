Kantō

Kantō

Are you looking for a city getaway in one of the most iconic countries in the world? Well then the Kantō region may just be the place you're looking for! Home to the capital city of Japan, Tokyo, Kantō is the perfect region for those who are interested in Japanese media and arts. Kantō has a population of roughly 43,509,179 people, making it a perfect place for making lifelong friends. Tokyo is a bustling city with a bright, exciting nightlife and contains some of Japan's most popular landmarks. It's a goldmine for those who are anime fans and is often a popular spot to visit.


The Kantō region is a geographical region of the largest island of Japan, Honshu. It is often known as the region that contains the greater Tokyo area and also seven prefectures: Chiba, Gunma, Ibaraki, Kanagawa, Saitama, Tochigi, and Tokyo. Since the Kantō region contains Tokyo, the region is also considered the political and economical center of Japan. The name Kantō literally means "East of the Barrier". However, nowadays it is generally considered to mean the region east.
